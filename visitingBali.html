visiting bali

|

entry requirement

there are a few things you'll need to enter bali

  • a passport thats valid for at least 6 months
  • apply for an e-visa or visa on arrival - apply in advance here or on arrival
  • pay the tourism levy (approx £7 / $13) - pay in advance here or on arrival
  • fill in the customs declaration form - complete up to 48 hours before your flight here or on arrival
  • complete a digital health pass - complete up to 48 hours before your flight here or on arrival

money

the currency in indonesia is the rupiah (IDR) and conversion is about 1 CAD = 12k IDR or 1 GBP = 20k IDR. we suggest getting a wise card and only carrying some cash at a time

power

bali uses type C and F (230V) power plugs, which are two-pin plugs like the ones found in europe

staying connected

we recommend either an eSIM through airalo (use REBECC1106 for a free credit) or a telkomsel SIM card if you want data while travelling in bali

getting around

taxis are readily available throughout bali, and if you’d like to book ahead, download grab, an app similar to uber. if you’re feeling adventurous, motorbikes are available for rent, usually through your hotel or airbnb

safety

bali is generally very safe for tourists, however it’s still important to be mindful of scams and theft, so don’t carry too much cash. you don’t need to worry about pickpockets, unless you’re around monkeys!

avoid bali belly

to prevent travel sickness, don’t drink the tap water - ice is fine because it’s regulated. you can take extra precautions like brushing your teeth with bottled water or taking probiotics, but it’s not necessary. also, be mindful if you choose to eat street food or local restaurants in rural areas!

seminyak

known for beach clubs and restaurants

beach clubs: potato head, mrs sippy, mano beach house, ku de ta

restaurants: kynd community (vg), motel mexicola (vf), uma garden (vf), hog wild

nusa dua

known for beaches (and sea sports)- 45 minutes from seminyak

highlights: pandawa and geger beaches, turtle conservation and education centre, bali national golf club

restaurants: koral (vf)

uluwatu

known for beaches (and surfing) - 1 hour from seminyak

highlights: suluban, ausin, and green bowl beaches, sundays beach club, uluwatu temple,

restaurants: bench brewery (vf), the cave (vf)

ubud

known for culture - 1 hour from seminyak

highlights (go early!)*: tirta empul temple, tegalalang rice terrace, tibumana waterfall, sangeh monkey forest

restaurants and cafes: ubud coffee roastery (vf), mudra cafe (vg), milk & madu (vf), keliki coffee (vf)

*there are plenty of affordable day trip tours from ubud, including private tours!

batukaru

known for eco-tourism and trekking - 1.5 hours from seminyak

highlights: mount batukaru, tanah lot temple, jatiluwih rice terraces, batukaru coffee estate

restaurants: the rustic (vf)

beyond mainland bali

small ferries from sanur can transport you to many nearby islands

highlights: nusa penida, lembongan, ceningan, gili islands

vg - vegan, vf - veggie friendly
